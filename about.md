Hi!
So this website is meant to be a combination blog, portfolio, and introduction to me, Melodie! I've made this to make some of my projects and thoughts more visible to anyone who might be interested in working with me! And uh... I'm trying to keep it relatively casual here. Sorry if that's not what you were looking for, but it's *my* website, after all.
So. A bit about me. I'm Melodie! My pronouns are she/they and I am a **white**, **queer**, **transgender**, **ADHD** engineer and designer. I got my BS in **mechanical engineering** from the University of Kentucky in 2023, and my MS in **robotics** from the University of Michigan in 2025. Since then, I've been living the life of a new grad, trying to find (or make) work for myself in southeast Michigan, where I currently live with my lovely partner. What that's looked like for me so far has been a whole lot of art! I play electronic music, I paint (especially miniatures), I run TTRPGs, and I do electronics hobby projects. I've also been figuring out a lot about my health, maybe even enough that I'll have to write a post about it...
Anyway, if you're here because you're considering hiring me, my email is the best place to reach me. I'm primarily interested in any entry-level positions where I can build on the amazing technical foundation I've already got. I'd love to work in a great breadth of fields, including **industrial automation**, **medical robotics**, **agricultural robotics**, **CNC machining**, **rapid prototyping and fabrication**, and more. I have a wide knowledge of subjects mechanical, electrical, and computer-related, and I'm certain that I can be a great addition to almost any engineering team. I have all the core skills to be a great engineer, and all I really need is a project to sink my teeth into.
Of course, my ideal interests would probably land me somewhere more in the **music technology** or **human-robot interaction** spaces, but I have to pay the bills somehow. Anyway, I can be satisfied with my personal projects in those areas. More to come on that!
In addition to all of this, I'm also a pretty active member of my community. I'm currently serving the people of Michigan by being a member of the Community Advisory Board for Planned Parenthood of Michigan's gender-affirming care program. This basically means I'm asked regularly about what Planned Parenthood absolutely **needs** to have in place for trans Michiganders to have a good experience. I love my community, and I value this work deeply. It's not the only way I'm involoved in my community, but it's probably the most official way.
